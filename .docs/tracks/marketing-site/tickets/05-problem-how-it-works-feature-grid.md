# 05: The problem, how it works, and the feature grid

**What to build:** The owner reads their own problem back in their own words, then sees how
collection actually works, then scans what the platform does. This is the
section where the temptation to round a number off is strongest, and where the
real reconciliation order earns more trust than a simplified one.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The problem section uses the customer's language, not the product's. No forbidden marketing words.
- [ ] The how-it-works matching step states the **real reconciliation order**: the account reference from the unit code, then the payer phone on file, then an exact amount match against open invoices, then the review queue.
- [ ] It states oldest invoice first, that an overpayment becomes a credit, and that an unmatched payment **posts to suspense immediately** — money in the ledger from the moment it arrives, just not yet attributed.
- [ ] A ten-card capability grid in which every card names its implementing service and carries an availability label, enforced by the type from ticket 03 (acceptance row 7).
- [ ] No claim in this section that cannot be traced to a written decision in the pack.
- [ ] Vocabulary is the glossary's: resident, landlord, organization, caretaker, agency. Never "tenant".
