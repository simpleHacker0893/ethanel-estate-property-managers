# 07: Role switcher, marketplace band and land band

**What to build:** An owner who only cares about one job can skip the rest, and can send a
colleague straight to the part that concerns them. This is also the ticket where
availability honesty is load-bearing: two of the bands describe work that is not
built and may never be.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The visitor switches the page to the view for their role — agency staff, landlord, resident, caretaker.
- [ ] The selected role is held in the URL, survives a refresh, and is shareable as a link.
- [ ] The switcher is keyboard operable and correctly announced, built on accessible tab primitives.
- [ ] The marketplace band states that the marketplace is later work rather than implying it is live.
- [ ] The land band states that plots and instalment sales sit in a sprint that is a **designated slip absorber and may not ship at all**, rather than selling week twelve as live.
- [ ] No map library loads. A static map image stands in for the marketplace visual.
